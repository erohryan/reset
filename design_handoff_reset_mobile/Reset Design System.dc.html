Assumptions: a desktop app plus a phone companion, with a physical NFC puck as the hero object. The puck's casing colour drives the app's accent, so each direction ships default casings and a custom hue. Every accent shares one lightness and chroma, and only the hue changes, so any casing stays easy on the eyes. Click a puck in any window or phone to simulate a tap, and pick a casing to re-theme.
